Act: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 & Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973
Section: Section 351 (Criminal Intimidation) & Section 78 (Stalking/Harassment)
Summary: Protection against personal harassment, threats, stalking, and criminal intimidation, right to file police complaint
Category: harassment

Every citizen has the legal right to protection against personal harassment, stalking, threats, extortion, and criminal intimidation under statutory criminal law. Any person experiencing persistent harassment, coercion, or threat to safety can preserve all relevant evidence (chat logs, call recordings, text messages, witness details), issue a clear written request to cease communication, and file a formal Non-Cognizable Report (NCR) or First Information Report (FIR) at the local police station or womens/citizens helpline. Police authorities are statutorily bound to receive and process complaints involving threat to safety.
