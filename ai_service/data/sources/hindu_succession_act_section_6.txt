Act: Hindu Succession Act, 1956
Section: Section 6
Summary: Equal coparcenary rights of daughters in a Joint Hindu family governed by Mitakshara law
Category: inheritance

A daughter of a coparcener in a Joint Hindu family governed by Mitakshara law becomes a coparcener by birth in the same manner as a son and has the same rights and liabilities in the coparcenary property. On a partition, a daughter is entitled to the same share as a son, subject to the statutory provisions and protections for certain dispositions, partitions and testamentary dispositions made before the specified cut-off date.
