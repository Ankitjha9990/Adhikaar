Act: Code on Wages, 2019
Section: Section 45
Summary: Claims arising from non-payment of wages, unauthorized deductions, and statutory compensation up to 10x
Category: workplace
Status: Current
Effective From: 2025-11-21

Under Section 45 of the Code on Wages, 2019, where an employee is paid less than the wages due, or where unauthorized deductions are made, or where wage payments are delayed, the aggrieved employee, any registered trade union of which the employee is a member, or an Inspector-cum-Facilitator may file an application before the designated Authority appointed by the appropriate Government. Under Section 45(2), the appointed Authority, after hearing the matter, may direct the employer to pay the full wages due together with such compensation as the Authority thinks fit, not exceeding ten times the amount of the claim determined.
