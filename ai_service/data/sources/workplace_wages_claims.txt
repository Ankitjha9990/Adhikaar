Act: Payment of Wages Act, 1936
Section: Section 15
Summary: Claims arising out of unauthorized deductions or delayed wages and statutory compensation (REPEALED)
Category: workplace
Status: Repealed
Effective Until: 2025-11-20
Replaced By: Code on Wages, 2019
Current Equivalent: Section 45

[HISTORICAL / REPEALED LEGISLATION]
Where any wages have been delayed or unauthorized deductions have been made, claims were formerly filed under Section 15 of Payment of Wages Act 1936. Note: The Payment of Wages Act, 1936 has been expressly repealed and subsumed under the Code on Wages, 2019 (effective 21 November 2025). Current wage claims, deduction disputes, and compensation must be filed under Section 45 of the Code on Wages, 2019 before the appointed Authority.
