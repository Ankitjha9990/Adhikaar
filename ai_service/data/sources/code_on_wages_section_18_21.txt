Act: Code on Wages, 2019
Section: Section 18 & Section 21
Summary: Permissible deductions from wages and restrictions on deductions for damage or loss
Category: workplace
Status: Current
Effective From: 2025-11-21

Under Section 18 of the Code on Wages, 2019, wages of an employee must be paid to them without deductions of any kind except those authorized by or under the Code. Under Section 21, deductions for damage to or loss of goods expressly entrusted to the employee, or for loss of money for which the employee is required to account, can only be made where such damage or loss is directly attributable to the employee's neglect or default. Furthermore, Section 18(3) mandates that total deductions authorized under the Code in any wage-period shall not exceed fifty per cent of such wages.
