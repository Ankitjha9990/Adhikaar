Act: Specific Relief Act, 1963 & Code of Civil Procedure, 1908
Section: Section 38 & Section 39
Summary: Civil remedies, restraining orders, and legal notices for personal or property interference
Category: general

Where an individual or entity interferes with a citizen's peaceful living, personal liberty, or property rights, the aggrieved person can issue a formal Legal Notice through a legal practitioner demanding an immediate end to the wrongful interference. If non-compliance continues, the person has the statutory civil right to file a suit for Injunction in the civil court of competent jurisdiction seeking a court order restraining the offending party.
