Act: Hindu Marriage Act, 1955
Section: Section 13B
Summary: Divorce by mutual consent for eligible spouses
Category: family

A petition for divorce by mutual consent may be presented jointly by both spouses where they have been living separately for a period of one year or more, have not been able to live together, and have mutually agreed that the marriage should be dissolved. The Act provides for a subsequent motion by both parties after the statutory waiting period specified in Section 13B, subject to the court being satisfied about the requirements of the provision.
