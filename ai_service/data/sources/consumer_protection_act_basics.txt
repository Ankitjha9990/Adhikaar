Act: Consumer Protection Act, 2019
Section: Section 2(7)
Summary: Definition of consumer rights and protection against defective goods or deficiency of service
Category: consumer

A consumer is any person who buys any goods or hires/avails any service for a consideration. Consumers have the statutory right to be protected against unfair trade practices, defective goods, and deficiency in services, and to seek replacement, refund, or correction from the seller or service provider.
