Act: Payment of Wages Act, 1936
Section: Section 5
Summary: Timely payment of wages and wage-period deadlines (REPEALED)
Category: workplace
Status: Repealed
Effective Until: 2025-11-20
Replaced By: Code on Wages, 2019
Current Equivalent: Section 17

[HISTORICAL / REPEALED LEGISLATION]
Provisions regarding timely payment of wages formerly fell under Section 5 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936. Note: The Payment of Wages Act, 1936 has been repealed and subsumed under the Code on Wages, 2019 (effective 21 November 2025). Current wage payment deadlines are governed by Section 17 of the Code on Wages, 2019.
