Act: Consumer Protection Act, 2019
Section: Section 34
Summary: Jurisdiction and procedure for filing a consumer complaint in District Commission
Category: consumer

A consumer complaint can be filed in the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (District Commission) within whose local limits of jurisdiction the complainant resides or personally works for gain, or where the cause of action arises. The complainant can file for redressal seeking refund, monetary compensation, or replacement of defective goods or deficient services.
