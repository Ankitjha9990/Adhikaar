Act: Legal Metrology Act, 2009
Section: Section 30
Summary: Penalty for transactions made in contravention of prescribed standards of weight or measure
Category: consumer

A person who, in relation to a transaction or deal, makes or uses a weight or measure that does not conform to the prescribed standard is liable to the penalty provided by Section 30 of the Act. The provision supports accurate measurement in commercial transactions and protects consumers and other parties from transactions based on non-standard weights or measures.
