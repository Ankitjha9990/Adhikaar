Act: Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987
Section: Section 12 & Section 13
Summary: Statutory right to free legal aid, legal advice, and representation for aggrieved citizens
Category: general

Under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, citizens facing legal disputes, harassment, rights violations, or financial hardship have the statutory right to free legal services, advice, and representation through District Legal Services Authorities (DLSA) and State Legal Services Authorities (SLSA). Aggrieved citizens can call the national legal aid helpline (15100), approach the nearest District Court Legal Services Committee, or file an application for free legal counsel to obtain formal legal remedies, protection orders, or legal notices.
