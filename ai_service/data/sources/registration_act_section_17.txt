Act: Registration Act, 1908
Section: Section 17
Summary: Documents for which registration is compulsory
Category: property

The section requires registration of specified documents, including instruments of gift of immovable property and certain non-testamentary instruments that create, declare, assign, limit or extinguish rights, title or interest in immovable property of the prescribed value. Certain other documents specified by the section are also subject to compulsory registration. The exact requirement depends on the nature of the document, the property and applicable statutory provisions, including relevant amendments.
